Scientific Preservation Reference

Conservation Protocols (ASI & ICOMOS)

Validated preservation procedures mapped to stone decay pathology across ancient granite and sandstone monument structures.

High Urgency ASI-PROT-FISSURE

Structural Fracture & Crack Intervention

Applicable for granite and sandstone masonry shear fissures & load fractures

1. Diagnostic Procedures

  • Conduct non-destructive Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) or Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) to determine internal fracture depth.
  • Mount calibrated polycarbonate tell-tale crack gauges with vernier scales to monitor active seasonal thermal or foundation displacement.
  • Examine foundation plinths for differential settlement, moisture heave, or tectonic shift.

2. Treatment & Stabilization

  • Flush fissure channels using oil-free low-pressure compressed air (1.5 bar) and deionized water.
  • Gravity injection of breathable Natural Hydraulic Lime grout (NHL 2.0 or NHL 3.5) blended with fine silica sand (1:2 ratio).
  • For severe structural cracks (>10mm), install Austenitic Grade 316 stainless steel helical stitching ties.
  • Point surface joints using sacrificial lime-pozzolana mortar matching original temple stone patina.
Strict Contraindication

DO NOT use Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) or epoxy resin injections. Rigid impermeable barriers accelerate differential thermal stress, leading to irreversible stone spalling and salt entrapment.

Medium Urgency ASI-PROT-BIOCIDE

Biological Colonization & Lichen Remediation

Applicable for algae, lichen, and bryophyte colonies on carved stone surfaces

1. Diagnostic Procedures

  • Identify biological species taxonomy and assess stone friability beneath fungal rhizoid anchors.
  • Measure surface dampness gradients using a non-invasive dielectric pinless moisture meter.
  • Inspect overhead tree canopy shade angles and microclimate humidity stagnation zones.

2. Biocidal Treatment

  • Pre-wet stone with demineralized water and gently brush with soft natural horsehair or tampico bristle brushes.
  • Apply biodegradable quaternary ammonium salt solution (2–3% w/v) poultice with 24–48 hour dwell time.
  • Nebulous low-pressure mist rinse (<3 bar) to clear loosened organic matter and spore residue.
  • Prune overhanging foliage to maintain minimum 2-meter air clearance for natural solar exposure and air circulation.
Strict Contraindication

DO NOT use high-pressure power washing (>15 bar) or wire brushes, which erode historic quarry tool marks and patina. Never apply sodium hypochlorite (household bleach) which introduces damaging hygroscopic salts.

High Urgency ASI-PROT-MOISTURE

Moisture Seepage & Efflorescence Control

Applicable for capillary rising damp and temple terrace water infiltration

1. Diagnostic Procedures

  • Map moisture thermal gradients across facade using calibrated infrared thermography to isolate water ingress sources.
  • Sample surface salt crystals for laboratory ion chromatography to identify damaging nitrates, chlorides, or sulfates.
  • Inspect temple roof parapet cornices, drainage gargoyles (pranala), and terrace waterproof lime layers.

2. Desalination & Waterproofing

  • Desalinate affected masonry using sacrificial cellulose pulp (Arbocel) poultices saturated with distilled water.
  • Re-grade terrace drainage channels to establish a minimum 1:50 slope away from structural vimana cores.
  • Apply breathable oligomeric silane/siloxane water-repellent impregnator only when substrate moisture is below 5%.
  • Install perimeter French gravel drains around temple plinth to intercept rising groundwater tables.
Strict Contraindication

DO NOT apply film-forming acrylic, polyurethane, or oil-based sealers. Sealing moisture within ancient stone cores causes sub-surface salt crystallization and catastrophic spalling.
